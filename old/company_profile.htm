Company Profile

1.   COMPANY - M/s. PRIMETECH INDUSTRIES

In the year 1991 the company started the venture under the Certification as Small Scale Industries with the aim of manufacturing and supplying heavy fabricated items and other precious fabricated jobs to various field of Engineering companies, as per their specific need and requirement. With Customer satisfaction as the prime motto and dream to create the broad client base with Quality rapport amongst valuable clients, the company invested their own resources with the double aim of the technological research and of Customers' full service by getting, year after year, a high degree of respectability.

M/s PRIMETECH INDUSTRIES having well-equipped manufacturing unit and machine shop, located at well-developed Industrial Estate at Vatva, Ahmedabad. The entire workshop is fully equipped with modern and sophisticated Machineries, well built in infrastructure setup including heavy E.O.T. Cranes to carry out big jobs. The company is well connected with National highway and approach road. The company possesses good team of professional Engineers and Commercial Staff to manage the manufacturing operation, Marketing, after Sales-service and Customer Care department etc.

2..   MAJOR ACTIVITIES:

The Company’s main field of activity is manufacturing of HIGH SPEED MIXER AND COOLER AND FLEXIBLE SPIRAL CONVEYORS.

Apart from manufacturing High Speed Mixer and Cooler and Flexible Spiral Conveyors, the company is also manufacturing post equipments for plastic extrusion machinery, other fabricated jobs. The company is also undertakaing projects for;

  1. Heavy fabrication work in M.S. and S.S. Construction
  2. Sheet Metal fabrication work in M.S. and S.S. Construction
  3. Mfg. Of special purpose machinery as per principal customers design.

The scope of work includes any type of fabrication, precision machinery parts as per Indian as well as International Standards. The jobs are manufactured based on the engineering practice as per ASME section VIII, div-I
