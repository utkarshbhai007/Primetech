High Speed Mixer and Cooler
In a High Speed mixer, the heat generated due to friction is utilised for mixing, hense it's known as "Heater mixer". High Speed mixers are used to mix solid to solid, liquid and pasty components. The heating phase in a heater mixer - it is not only the shortest possible cycle time which is the decisive factor, but in many cases, the way is which friction is introduced in to the product is of far greater importance.
